01. About the Developer

Fahad Umrani is a web and Python developer based in Pakistan. His principal project is Umrani AI, an AI chat web application that operates entirely on the client side: several AI providers with automatic failover, streaming responses, and conversation history stored locally in the browser through IndexedDB — with no application server.

His second flagship project is a Blockchain-Based Voting System, developed as a final-year academic project. It combines citizen records, live-camera face verification, and tamper-evident commit–reveal voting on the Ethereum Sepolia network, and was verified through complete end-to-end testing of both ballots on-chain.

He is a second-year student at a Cadet College, pursuing his intermediate education alongside his development work.
