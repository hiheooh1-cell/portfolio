01. About the Developer

Fahad Umrani is a web and Python developer based in Pakistan. His principal project is Umrani AI — an AI chat app that runs entirely in the browser, with several AI providers, automatic failover and streaming responses.

His second flagship project is a Blockchain-Based Voting System, a final-year academic project with live-camera face verification and commit–reveal voting on Ethereum Sepolia, tested end to end on-chain.

He is a second-year student at a Cadet College, studying alongside his development work.
